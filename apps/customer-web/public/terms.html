What the service is

Easy Buy Delivery connects customers with independent riders for three kinds of errand: Food (ordering from a listed store), Pabili (a rider buys items on your behalf) and Padala (a rider carries a parcel from one place to another). We coordinate the delivery. We do not manufacture, prepare or sell the goods themselves, and we are not the merchant.
